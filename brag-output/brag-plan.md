# Brag Plan: StatVidya (स्टैटविद्या) — 20s Launch Video

## What is this app?
StatVidya is an AI-powered skill intelligence and competency-based capacity building platform engineered for India's Official Statistical System (MoSPI & NSSTA under Mission Karmayogi, Problem SIH 26101).

## The Problem & Solution
- **Problem**: Identifying competency gaps across India's vast statistical workforce (ISS/SSS cadres, NSSO FOD field enumerators, State DES).
- **Solution**: Automated competency profiling mapped to the FRAC framework, targeted learning recommendations via the iGOT Karmayogi ecosystem, and automated AI assessment generation from official statistical manuals.
- **Who it helps**: Civil servants, government officials, and national policy leadership.
