# Brag Plan: StatVidya (स्टैटविद्या)

## What is this app?
StatVidya is a domain-grounded workforce competency intelligence platform engineered for India's Official Statistical System (MoSPI & NSSTA under Mission Karmayogi, SIH 26101), connecting offline field assessments to national data quality analytics.

## The angle
Sovereign Civic Tech meets Silicon Valley craft. This isn't another generic quiz app; it's the intelligent competency nervous system for 10M+ civil servants across India. It solves the hardest problem in public statistics: ensuring that field enumerators in remote villages and senior directors in New Delhi are aligned on rigorous statistical standards, with an offline-first Hindi PWA that guarantees zero data loss.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast cinematic opening.
Deep navy backdrop (`#1F273A`). A glowing Ashoka-inspired Karmayogi Emblem pulses into view:
**"India's decisions depend on statistical data."**
Quick cut on the kick drum (0:02.5):
**"Who ensures the data is flawless?"**
Text snaps: **"Meet StatVidya."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Live Competency Radar**: An interactive geometric radar chart animating L1–L5 proficiency across official FRAC domains (Field Surveying, Data Scrutiny, Sampling Theory, APAR Readiness).
- **Offline Field-First PWA (NSSO FOD)**: A mock tablet frame showing Sunita Devi's bilingual (Hindi/English) field survey assessment runner operating seamlessly with "Offline · 0 Data Loss" status badge.
- **AI Survey Manual MCQ Engine**: Drag-and-drop MoSPI statistical manual PDF $\to$ Gemini 3.6 Flash instantaneously generates peer-reviewed assessment items with verified provenance citations.
- **Leadership Executive Intelligence**: Rajesh Kumar's (ADG) macro command desk showing real-time correlation: "Training Hours $\uparrow 34\% \implies$ Survey Error Rate $\downarrow 42\%$".

## Outro / punchline
A sharp, proud resolution:
**"From Village Field Surveys to National Policy."**
The official emblem settles with Ministry Navy (`#1C4CA1`) and Saffron Orange (`#FFA72F`) glows:
**"StatVidya · Competency Intelligence for MoSPI & NSSTA"**
Sub-caption: *Engineered for Smart India Hackathon 2024 / Mission Karmayogi (SIH 26101)*.

## User flow worth showing
**Entry $\to$ Key Action $\to$ Result:**
1. **Entry**: Sunita Devi enters an NSSO FOD field survey assessment in rural Bihar on a low-bandwidth tablet with native Hindi interface.
2. **Key Action**: Completes an offline agricultural survey assessment with instant local validation and IndexedDB sync queue.
3. **Result**: Upon connection, competency scores sync seamlessly to MoSPI HQ, unlocking targeted iGOT Karmayogi learning pathways and updating the National Cadre Readiness Index.
